1/6 🚀 Introducing **Horizon-Veridian**: Closed-loop sprint governance for engineering teams powered by @Gnani_AI & LangGraph.

Tired of meeting bots spamming Jira mid-call? Here's how we solved autonomous meeting execution with strict manager oversight: 🧵👇

2/6 🎙️ **Multilingual Meeting Ingestion:**
Meetings conducted in conversational Hinglish are ingested and transcribed with precise speaker diarization via Gnani Prisma v3.0. Zero manual note-taking.

3/6 🛡️ **4-Way Compliance Firewall:**
Before ANY ticket is touched, our LangGraph firewall validates:
• Cloud budget thresholds
• Security policies & blocked APIs (SEC-POL-104)
• HR Leave records (Smart Leave Redirection to lowest-workload peers!)

4/6 🛑 **Zero Tickets Pre-Approval:**
No mid-meeting ticket clutter. The Engineering Manager gets an interactive Block Kit Review Menu in a private Slack DM. Nothing enters Jira until approved.

5/6 💬 **Individual 1-on-1 DMs & Evidence-Based Auto-Close:**
Approved tasks dispatch to Jira SCRUM and DM the assigned engineer.
When the engineer replies with a PR link or commit hash, the Jira ticket auto-closes to Done!

6/6 📊 100% accuracy on our 12-sample Hinglish evaluation benchmark.
Built with Next.js 15, FastAPI, LangGraph, Supabase pgvector & Slack/Jira Cloud APIs.
